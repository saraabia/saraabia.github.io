/ 02 / The winning experiment, prototyped

The Sunday Recap card. Generate a real one below.

The hypothesis: Extra's pitch is "give back hours every week." But users don't feel hours — they feel small moments of relief. Quantifying the time saved at the right emotional moment (Sunday evening, before the week resets) should drive a measurable lift in week-2 retention and share rate.

Below is a working prototype of what the card would look like inside Extra. Adjust the inputs to simulate different user profiles, then hit "Generate recap" to see the rendered card.

// Simulate a user week

The card uses a simple time-saved estimate: 90 seconds per message Extra handled silently. That's deliberately conservative — most inbox time is decision fatigue, not reading. Even at this estimate, a heavy user sees hours saved within a single week.

The full test design

Hypothesis Quantifying time saved + surfacing it at end of week 1 lifts week-2 retention by ≥8% and produces ≥5% organic share rate.
Setup Two-cell A/B over 14 days on new signups. Control: standard onboarding. Treatment: same onboarding + Sunday recap at end of week 1.
Sample size 500 per cell minimum (scaled up depending on current new-user volume; current launch traction suggests this is comfortably achievable in ~5 days).
Primary metric Week-2 retention (DAU on day 8–14 ÷ signups in cohort).
Secondary Share button taps, in-app referral installs, "would you recommend" prompt response on day 14.
Guardrail Unsubscribe rate on the recap email itself (must stay below 4%). We don't want to become the noise we're killing.
Ship / kill criteria Ship: retention lift ≥8% with p < 0.1, OR share rate ≥5%. Iterate: 3–8% lift, run a copy variant test. Kill: <3% lift AND share <2%.
