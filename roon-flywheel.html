/ 01 / The flywheel

Four stages, one self-reinforcing loop. Click any stage to see the agentic workflow underneath it.

Physician onboarding is rarely about acquisition alone. The loop only compounds when sourced doctors activate, activated doctors retain, and retained doctors refer their network back into the top. Each stage below is an AI-assistable workflow Roon could run with a small team.

01 / Source

Identify the right physicians, faster.

Surface high-fit specialists by institution, subspecialty, and content adjacency.

02 / Reach

Personalized, time-respectful outreach.

Multi-touch messaging that lands in doctors' very limited time windows.

03 / Activate

Onboard them to first contribution.

From signup to first published video or post in under two weeks.

04 / Refer

Turn one physician into ten.

Active physicians' networks are the highest-converting acquisition channel.

/ 03 / What I'd ship in week one

A small, real experiment. Reported back by Friday. The same week.

Less talking. More doing. Below is a five-day plan I'd execute in my first week at Roon to test one specific hypothesis about physician outreach. End-of-week deliverable: a Loom + a one-pager + a recommendation.

Mon Frame the hypothesis

Sit with the growth lead. Pick one specialty (e.g. dermatology, where Roon's library is still building). Hypothesis: "Endocrinologists who follow at least 2 other Weill Cornell physicians on Roon are 3x more likely to onboard within 14 days." Define the metric. Set the sample size.

Output: Notion doc, one paragraph hypothesis, defined success metric.

Tue Build the experiment surface

In Airtable + Claude: pull 30 candidate physicians fitting the criteria. Use the outreach agent above to draft 30 personalized messages, each based on their published work. Stage them for human review before send.

Output: 30 reviewable drafts, sorted by predicted-fit score.

Wed Send + measure

Send the cohort in batches. Track every signal: opens, replies, click-through to onboarding video, sign-up. Pipe events from Customer.io into a single Notion table. Watch the funnel hourly the first 48 hours.

Output: live funnel dashboard, real-time response rate.

Thu Run the comparison

Match the 30-physician cohort against the prior 30 from the control workflow (Roon's standard outreach). Calculate the lift, error bars, and confidence. Use Claude to draft the writeup. Cross-check the math myself.

Output: comparison table, statistical confidence note.

Fri Report + recommend

One-page memo with the result, three plain-English learnings, and one specific recommendation for next week: which outreach pattern to scale, which to kill, what to test next. Share in #growth-and-ops with a 90-second Loom walkthrough.

Output: shipped Loom + memo, ready for Vikram and the team.
